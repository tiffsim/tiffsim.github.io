    <p>
      I am a political scientist and postdoctoral research fellow at the Middle East Initiative at Harvard Kennedy School. My research focuses on Middle East politics, governance, and political behavior.
    </p>

    <p>
      My primary research examines how states in the Arab Gulf use leisure, culture, and wellbeing as tools of governance, and how these policies shape political satisfaction, participation, and state-society relations. I also study civic participation and public service delivery in Pakistan and how governance preferences are shaped by migration to the Gulf. 
    </p>
    
    <p>
    Aross these projects, I use large-scale text analysis, field and survey experiments, and qualitative research to understand how people's everyday experiences with the state shape political behavior.
